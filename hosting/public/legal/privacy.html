1. Overview

La Nonna helps families share a baby’s journey—photos, calendar, registry, announcements, and invitations—with a trusted circle. This policy describes personal information we process when you use the Android app (com.lanonna.lanonna) and our backend services. The app is intended for adults; it stores information about babies and children that you add. We do not offer child accounts.

2. Information we collect

You provide: account email and profile fields (name, avatar, optional phone, birth date, country, postal code); baby profile and family content (photos, events, registry, announcements, comments); invitee emails; notification preferences.

Automatic: Firebase user ID; FCM push token when notifications are enabled; throttled last-seen timestamp; Firebase App Check signals; crash logs via Firebase Crashlytics when enabled (may include your Firebase UID).

Calendar and registry “AI Suggestions” use bundled catalogs on your device, not a third-party generative AI API. Photos may use camera or library access; images are processed on-device before upload.

3. How we use information

We use data to authenticate you, operate family sharing, send notifications and invitation emails, fulfill export requests, secure the service, and improve stability. We use Google Cloud and Firebase to host the service and Mailjet to send invitation emails.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data with family members you authorize, with service providers (Google/Firebase, Mailjet) under contract, and when required by law. Invite pages may be served from https://{{DOMAIN}}/.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain data while your account is active. Pending invites expire per schedule. When you delete your account in the app, we tombstone your profile, delete your Firebase login, end memberships, revoke invites, soft-delete baby profiles you solely own, remove certain participation data, and revoke biometric credentials. Some content you created may remain for other members; cloud media may not all be removed automatically. Contact {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} for questions.

6. Your choices

  • Edit profile and notification settings in the app.
  • Delete account: Account → Delete account → confirm and re-authenticate if prompted.
  • Export baby data (owners): Account → Export baby data → request export → download when ready.

Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights. Contact {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

7. Children

The Service is not directed at children to use as account holders. Parents and guardians provide information about minors. Consult your lawyer regarding COPPA, GDPR child data, and store audience settings.

8. Security and changes

We use HTTPS, access controls, and industry-standard practices. We may update this policy at https://{{DOMAIN}}/legal/privacy with a new effective date.
